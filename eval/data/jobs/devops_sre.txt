Site Reliability Engineer

Our platform team keeps a multi-region SaaS product running for millions of users. We're hiring an SRE to strengthen reliability and automation.

Responsibilities
- Operate and scale Kubernetes clusters on AWS
- Manage infrastructure as code with Terraform
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, Helm)
- Improve monitoring and alerting with Prometheus and Grafana; lead incident response and postmortems
- Automate operational work with Python or Bash

Requirements
- 4+ years in SRE, DevOps, or infrastructure engineering
- Strong Linux fundamentals and networking knowledge
- Production Kubernetes, Terraform, and AWS experience
- Experience with observability tooling and on-call operations
