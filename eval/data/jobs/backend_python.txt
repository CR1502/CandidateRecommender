Senior Backend Engineer (Python)

We're a fintech company building payment infrastructure used by thousands of merchants. You'll join a team of eight engineers who own our core APIs.

What you'll do
- Design, build, and operate high-throughput REST APIs in Python (FastAPI and Django)
- Model data in PostgreSQL and tune queries for performance
- Run services on Kubernetes in AWS, with Docker-based CI/CD pipelines
- Mentor mid-level engineers and lead technical design reviews
- Take part in the on-call rotation for the services you own

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, mostly in Python
- Deep experience with FastAPI or Django and PostgreSQL
- Production experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS
- Solid understanding of API design, testing, and observability

Nice to have
- Experience with Redis, Celery, or Kafka
- Background in payments or financial systems
